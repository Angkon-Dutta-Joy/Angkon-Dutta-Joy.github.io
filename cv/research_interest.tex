\section{Research Interests}

\begin{itemize}

    \item \textbf{Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning}: Deep learning, generative models, representation learning, and intelligent systems.

    \item \textbf{Biomedical AI and Signal Processing}: Machine learning for biomedical signals, particularly EEG analysis and reconstruction.

    \item \textbf{Computer Vision}: Vision-based human activity analysis, anatomical keypoint modeling, and temporal deep learning.

    \item \textbf{Data Science}: Data-driven modeling, predictive analytics, and machine learning for complex real-world datasets.

    \item \textbf{Human--Computer Interaction}: Intelligent and user-centered systems, with an interest in applying AI to improve human--computer interaction.

    \item \textbf{Trustworthy and Interpretable AI}: Developing reliable, interpretable, and meaningful machine learning systems.

\end{itemize}
