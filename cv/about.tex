\section{About Me}

I am a Computer Science graduate from \href{https://www.bracu.ac.bd/}{BRAC University}, Bangladesh, where I completed my \textbf{B.Sc. in Computer Science and Engineering} with a CGPA of \textbf{3.82/4.00}. My research interests lie at the intersection of \textbf{Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, Computer Vision, Biomedical Computing, Signal Processing, and Spatiotemporal Modeling}. My undergraduate thesis, \textit{Diffusion Models with Graph Attention for Spatiotemporal EEG Inpainting}, explored a hybrid diffusion--graph attention framework for reconstructing incomplete multichannel EEG signals, under the supervision of \href{https://www.bracu.ac.bd/about/people/md-golam-rabiul-alam-phd}{Dr. Md. Golam Rabiul Alam} and co-supervision of \href{https://www.bracu.ac.bd/about/people/swakkhar-shatabda-phd}{Dr. Swakkhar Shatabda}. I also co-authored an ICCIT 2025 paper on vision-based fall detection using anatomical keypoints and temporal deep learning. I am currently seeking graduate research opportunities where I can further develop my research experience and contribute to meaningful work in AI and related fields.
