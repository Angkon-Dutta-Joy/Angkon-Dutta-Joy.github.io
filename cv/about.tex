\section{About Me}

I am a Computer Science graduate from \href{https://www.bracu.ac.bd/}{BRAC University}, Bangladesh, where I completed my \textbf{B.Sc. in Computer Science and Engineering} with a CGPA of \textbf{3.82/4.00}. My research interests primarily focus on \textbf{Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning}, particularly \textbf{Deep Learning, Generative AI, Computer Vision, Biomedical AI, Signal Processing, and HCI}. My undergraduate thesis, \textit{Diffusion Models with Graph Attention for Spatiotemporal EEG Inpainting}, explored a hybrid diffusion--graph attention framework for reconstructing incomplete multichannel EEG signals using \textbf{Denoising Diffusion Probabilistic Models (DDPM), Graph Attention Networks (GAT), and Singular Value Decomposition (SVD)}. The work received the \textbf{Best Thesis Award at BRAC University in 2025}.

I also co-authored a research paper on \textbf{vision-based fall detection using anatomical keypoints and temporal deep learning}, published at \textbf{ICCIT 2025}. Beyond research, I have experience in AI-focused training, robotics, and research-oriented activities through the BRAC University Computer Club, including serving as a trainer for the \textbf{Bangladesh Artificial Intelligence Olympiad (BdAIO) 2025} and conducting a session on \textbf{Diffusion Models} at the \textbf{IAIO 2026 Grooming Camp} at BRAC University. I am currently working as a \textbf{Research Assistant} at BRAC University under the supervision of \textbf{Md. Golam Rabiul Alam, PhD}, and am seeking graduate research opportunities where I can further develop my research expertise and contribute to impactful work in AI and related fields.
